# Soap Opera Sequence — 5 emails, the re-introduction arc

Internal. Real copy, ready to load into `scripts/reactivation/email-templates.mjs`.

**Audience:** the 191 email-reachable people from Judah's list, imported
2026-09-15 with verbal consent collected 2026-09-10. Most last gave between
2013 and 2017. They are **lapsed supporters, not cold prospects** — they gave
money to this mission once, and the writing has to respect that or it insults
them.

**Relationship to `email-templates.md`:** that file is the 6-email, 14-day
*reactivation* sequence. This is Brunson's 5-email *re-introduction* arc and
runs **first**. Reactivation assumes they remember who Judah is; after eight
years of silence, most do not. Run this, then that, or run this alone and fold
its CTA into the cohort ask.

**Frameworks applied:** Brunson's Soap Opera Sequence for structure; the
Influence Protocol for the inside of each email — Cialdini levers marked per
email, Hormozi's offer variables carried in Email 5, Carnegie's no-criticism
and genuine-appreciation rules throughout.

---

## The honesty constraint, read this before editing

A Soap Opera Sequence runs on a real story with a real low point. **I do not
know Judah's.** Email 2 is where that belongs, and inventing one — for a
501(c)(3) writing to people who have given it money — is not a copywriting
shortcut, it is a lie to donors.

So every place the story needs a fact only Judah has, the copy carries a
`{{JUDAH: ...}}` prompt instead of invented detail. **Those are not optional
placeholders like `{{first_name}}`. An email that ships with one still in it is
not finished.**

Everything outside those brackets is grounded in what the record already
supports: Messianic Jew, ordained minister, licensed counselling therapist,
~50 years combined practice, 24+ countries, Change A Destiny founded 2008,
Victorious Villages running in 3 nations including Cambodia, the book at $9.10,
and a Founding Cohort that has not launched.

**Guardrails, from the framework's own rules and `offer-stack.md`:** no
manufactured scarcity — the cohort cap is whatever Judah's real calendar allows
and nothing else. No health, income, or outcome claims. No countdown that isn't
a real date.

## Email 1 — Day 0 — "Set the stage"

**Job:** explain the silence before asking for anything. Earn the next open.
**Levers:** Unity (shared mission), Liking (candour), Reciprocity (gives a story,
asks nothing).

**Subject:** Eight years is a long time to say nothing
**Preview:** No ask in this one. I owe you an explanation first.

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> You gave to Change A Destiny once. Depending on when, that was somewhere
> between eight and twelve years ago — and you have heard almost nothing from
> me since.
>
> That is my fault, not an accident, and I want to explain it properly over the
> next few emails rather than pretend the gap did not happen.
>
> Here is the short version: the work never stopped. Victorious Villages is
> running in three nations. The model you helped start — a village that becomes
> self-sustaining rather than permanently supported — is still the model.
>
> What stopped was me telling anyone about it.
>
> I will explain why in the next email. It is not a flattering story, but you
> paid for the right to hear it.
>
> Judah
>
> P.S. If you would rather not hear from me at all, the unsubscribe link below
> works immediately and I will not take it personally. You gave when it
> mattered; that does not obligate you to anything now.

**Levers:** Liking (vulnerability), Authority (earned, not asserted), Unity.

**Subject:** {{JUDAH: 4–7 words naming the hard thing, plainly. Not clickbait.}}
**Preview:** The part I have not told supporters.

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> I said I would explain the silence.
>
> {{JUDAH: The actual story. What happened, roughly when, and what it cost —
> burnout, a loss, a failure in the work, a season where the ministry or your
> own faith came apart. One specific scene beats five general sentences: where

**Levers:** Authority (dual credential — the strongest asset in the business),

**Preview:** Fifty years of both, and they never once spoke to each other.

Every email carries at least four levers across the arc, and no email carries
scarcity except the one with a real constraint behind it.

## Objections, and where each is answered

1. *"Why are you emailing me after eight years?"* — Email 1, first line, before
   anything is asked for.
2. *"Is this a fundraising appeal in disguise?"* — Emails 1–4 ask for nothing;
   Email 5 sells a course, and the donation ask is deliberately absent from this
   whole sequence (`offer-stack.md`: one funnel, one job).
3. *"Another self-help book."* — Email 3 leads with the dual credential, which
   is the one thing no competitor has.
4. *"I do not have time."* — Email 5 states the real time cost before the price.
5. *"What if it does not work for me?"* — Email 5, refund in the first two weeks.
