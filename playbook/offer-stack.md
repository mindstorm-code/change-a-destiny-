# Offer Stack — The Path / Change A Destiny

Internal strategy reference. Not customer-facing — nothing here ships to
the public site.

## Value ladder (Brunson)

1. **Entry — Free Path Assessment.** 3-question self-assessment ("which
   hurt, hang-up, or habit is actually running your calendar?"). Zero
   friction, zero payment. Job: convert a stranger into an email address
   and a segment (Purpose-curious vs. Passion-curious).
2. **Self-liquidating offer — The Path (Book 1).** $9.10, already
   published (ASIN B0HCFBDL13). Job: recoup acquisition cost and prove the
   framework in a low-risk format before asking for real money.
3. **Core cash offer — The Path: Transformative Living (Founding Cohort).**
   $497–$1,497 depending on tier. Job: the actual business model. Real
   cohort, real start date, real seat cap tied to Judah's actual bandwidth
   for live calls — not manufactured scarcity.
4. **Ascension — Change A Destiny monthly partnership.** Introduced
   *inside* the course (module 6, "Passion in Action"), not competed for
   attention on the landing page. Healed people become generous people;
   this is proof-driven, not guilt-driven.
5. **Continuity — Path Community.** Low-cost ($19–$29/mo) ongoing
   membership for course graduates who want to stay in relationship after
   the cohort ends. Not yet built; flag as a phase-2 add once cohort 1 has
   run and produced real testimonials.
6. **Referral — Path Guides.** Graduates who want to lead a small group
   through the book/course become an unpaid or lightly-incentivized
   referral channel, and a farm system for future keynote/organizational
   bookings.

## Offer stack strength (Hormozi)

- **Outcome:** measurable emotional/spiritual resilience + a concrete next
  action for justice (giving), not vague "transformation."
- **Likelihood of success:** Judah's dual credential (licensed therapist +
  ordained minister, 50 years combined practice) is the single strongest
  belief-lever in the whole business — every asset should surface it early
  and often.
- **Speed to result:** the book is a same-day win; the course is a 6-week
  win. Both should be stated as concrete timeframes on the page, not
  implied.
- **Effort/sacrifice:** 15–20 minute video modules, one live call/week —
  explicitly designed to fit around a schedule already full of ministry.

## One funnel, one job (applied here)

The flagship landing page has **one** primary job: move a stranger toward
the Founding Cohort (with the book as the natural fallback/lower-commitment
option on the same page). Change A Destiny appears as **proof and
narrative**, not a second competing ask — a visitor should never have to
choose between "give money" and "buy the course" on the same screen. That
split-attention problem was the main structural flaw in the first draft of
this site.

## Validation angle

The course has not launched yet — there is genuine uncertainty about
willingness to pay at these price points. Treat the "Founding Cohort" as
the real validation funnel: a real reserve-your-seat mechanism with a real
seat cap and a real note about what happens next (a follow-up email when
enrollment opens), not a fake countdown or a "buy now" button that goes
nowhere. If reservations don't materialize at a reasonable rate against
real traffic, that's the answer, not a sign to reword the headline again.
