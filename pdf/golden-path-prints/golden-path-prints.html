How to print

  • Print only the pages you want. Each piece is two pages: with words, then art alone.
  • Choose landscape and "fit to page." Pick "borderless" for the art-only pages if your printer offers it.
  • Heavier matte paper or card stock makes them look framed even without a frame.
  • They fit a standard 8.5 × 11 frame. The art-only pages also trim well to 8 × 10.
